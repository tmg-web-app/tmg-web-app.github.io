## Opportunity Notes for TMG's Site

1. **Lead with desktop software.** It's the only capability no competitor claims (WD's is web/SaaS only). "Web + Software + Multimedia under one roof" as the H1, not buried as the third service card.
2. **Publish price anchors.** Even a "websites starting at $X / monthly maintenance at $Y" page differentiates against 6/6 quote-walls.
3. **Show recency signals everywhere.** Current copyright year, dated portfolio entries, and any blog cadence immediately outrivals 4 of these 6 sites (Safari, Melcro, JJN, b.a).
4. **LP/audit wedge.** b.a's productized "website audit" and WP-rescue offer is a proven low-friction lead generator this size class uses — TMG could productize something similar.
5. **Name your clients.** Every strong competitor leans on roster social proof (JJN's WV institutions; Thundera's Oshkosh Defense). TMG's portfolio currently has minimal named proof.
